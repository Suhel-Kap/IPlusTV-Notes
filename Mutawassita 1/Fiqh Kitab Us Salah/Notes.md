**Masla:** Namaz ke liye dil se pukhta irada zaroori hai. Mai konsi namaz padh raha hu ye dil me sochna zaroori hai ya nahi?

Sheikh ke kareeb raaye, Sheikh Ibn Uthaymeen ne bhi isi raaye ko sahi karar diya hai - aapke upar jo chiz wajib hai wo chiz ki bas generic niyat karna kaafi hogi. Eg asr ka waqt ho toh bas itna niyat karna ki me farz namaz padh raha hu kaafi hoga.

Namaz shuru hui toh pata chala kisi aur ne namaz padhayi, toh iss surat me namaz ki sehat par koi asar nahi padega. 

**Qaeda:** Koi bhi ibadat kar rahe ho usme uss ibadat ki sifat ki niyat karna shart nahi hai, asl ki niyat karna kaafi hai. Eg. kisi aadmi ki namaz chhoot jaaye sone ki wajah se, usne niyat kiya ki me namaz ada kar raha hu. Qaza ki niyat nahi ki usne. Iss surat me namaz ho jayegi kyuki ada/qadha sifat hai. 

**Masla:** Har wo ibadat jiski farz aur nafil dono hone ki possibility hai, aisi surat me farz/niyat ki niyat karna zaroori hai. Eg. fajr ki namaz, nafil aur farz dono same hai, toh isme khas niyat karna zaroori hai. Again an eg, koi aadmi ghar se nikal asr ki namaz ke liye azan hone ke baad aur masjib pohcha toh jamaat khadi hui hai, toh uske liye bas itna niyat karna ki me farz padh raha hu wo kaafi hai.

Lekin darmiyan me agar raste me koi kaam yaad aagaya, kaam nipta ke fir masjid gaya - toh usko iss surat me wapis niyat karna zaroori hai. 

**Masla:** Niyat kahan se karna hai?

Agar koi zubaan se niyat kare namaz shuru karne se pehle toh kam-as-kam ye bidat hai. 

**Masla:** Jo bolke karta hai, unhe toka jaaye, toh wo bolte hai - Nabi SAWS ne jab hajj kiya toh jab ihram banda toh zubaan se bol karke talbiyah bola. 
**Jawab:** Talbiyah sunnat hai, iska taa'luk niyat se nahi hai.

### Lesson 26

at tashriko fin-niyatul ma'mul lahu ki 3rd type:
- Allah ke liye ibadat kar rha hai aur sath sath me kisi dunyawi chiz ka fayda bhi talab kar raha hai. iski 2 kismein hai
	1. agar fayda esa hai jisse shariat ne iska zikr kiya ho, allowed ho. 
	   eg., aap SAWS ne bola ke agar koi chahta ho rizq me barqat chahta hai toh wo chahiye ke silah rehmi kare. aadmi silah-rehmi karte time maksad ho ke rizq me barkat chahiye toh ye bilkul jayez hai.
	   koi aadmi sadqah kar rha hai aur soch raha hai ke sadqe se duniya me shifa **bhi** mile toh ye bilkul jayez hai. aap SAWS ne bola apne bimaaro ki shifa sadqe se karo.
	   agar koi hajj kar raha hai aur soche ke raste me tijarat etc karke fayda uthaye toh isme bhi koi harj ki baat nahi hai.
	   agar koi khaalis Allah ki raza ke liye kare toh jo fayde hai wo usko automatically milega
	2. agar shariat me koi esa indication nahi ho ke duniyawi fayda milega. toh kya ye jayez hai? ikhtilaf hai - 
	   a. agar uss ibadat ko karne ka asal sabab raza mandi hasil karna hai toh ye jayez hai but not the other way around. eg koi imam hai, iska koi duniyawi fayada nahi bataya gaya hai aur wo soche ke me iske liye tankhwa bhi le le hatta ke tankhwa band ho jaye to bhi wo imamamt karega toh ye jayez hai
	   b. agar ikhlas ghalib hai toh jayez hai aur agar duniyawi fayda ghalib ho toh jayez nahi hai
