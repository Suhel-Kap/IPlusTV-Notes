#### Shuroot us'salah

Shart wo chiz hai jo nahi payi jaye toh jo hukm hai wo bhi nahi paya jayega, lekin uske paye jane se zaroori nahi hai ke hukm paya jaaye 

discussion shuroot us'sehhah aur ul'ijzah se hoga

namaz ke shuroot us'sehhah ke 2 types hai:
1. shart ejabi
	- jis kaam ke karne ka hukm ho aur wo shart ho
	- kisi kaam ko karne ka hukm hota hai
	- eg. taharat, qibla face
2. shart a'dmi
	- koi esa kaam jisse bachne ka hukm diya ja raha hai
	- eg. najazat se bachna

**IMP:** dono sharton ke hukm ke farq:
1. ejabi ko jaane/unjaane me chhod kar namaz padh liya toh count nai hoti
2. a'dmi ka hukm ye hai ke agar unjaane me hui toh namaz valid hai (eg, agar najasat lagi hai aur khabar nahi ho) lekin agar jaante hue kari toh sahi nahi hogi 

shart, wajib aur ruqn ko kisi ne jaan ke chhoda toh uski ibadat sahi nahi hai - ye 3no wajib hai

ruqn ko jaane/anjaane me chhodne se namaz nahi hogi. eg agar aap 3rd rakat me ho and ruqn reh gaya toh wo raqat invalid hai and you need to do one extra correct raqat. aur agar namaz ke bahar yaad aaya toh namaz dohrani hogi.

agar wajib galti se chhoda ho toh namaz valid hai but sajdah sahuh karna hoga. jaan ke chhoda toh namaz baatil hai.

- shart agar galti se chhoda hai, toh check karna hoga ek ejabi ya a'dmi ko chhoda hai (refer above)
- jo chizen shart hai namaz ke liye wo actual namaz se pehle hoti hai. daakhil hone ke baad ruqn start hote hai. 
- jo chiz shart hoti hai wo namaz ke start se end tak paya jana zaroori hai, eg wudu, istikbal e qibla, awrah ka dhakna.
- arqan namaz ki maahiyat ka hissa hote hai. meaning - inhi arqan se zariye se namaz muraqqab hota hai. shart namaz ke bahar hi chiz hai.
- namaz ke sunnan chhodne se namaz valid rehti hai.

## Shuroot Us'Salah

Namaz ke sahi hone ke liye 9 shartein hai. Ye sab hona zaroori hai namaz sahi hone ke liye. ek bhi nahi hone se namaz nahi hogi.

1. Islam
	- jab muad ibn jabal RA ko yemen bheja tab pehle unko shadatayn ki dawat ke liye bola aur fir namaz ke liye bola nabi SAWS ne
	- ibadat ki qabooliyat ki 2 shartein hai - ikhalas aur mutaba'at
	- qabooliyat ki 2 kismein hai:
		a. qabool sehat aur ijzah - kya humari ibadat sahi hai ke nahi, aur kya kaafi hai ke nai
		b. qabool sawab wa jaza 
	abdullah ibn umar se riwayat hai - 2 log ese hai jinki namazein unko sar se upar nahi jaati: 1. esa gulam jo aaka ko chhod ke bhag gaya hoga 2. esi aurat jo shohar ki nafarmani kare.
	Agar kisi ki namaz sahi nahi hogi toh keh sakte hia usko ajaro-sawab nahi milega, lekin agar kisi ki namaz sahi hogi usse ye laazim nahi hota ke usko ajaro-sawab milega.
2. Aqal 
	- ye: 
		- shart-e-ujoob - uspe fard nai hai
		- shart-e-sehat - padh le toh sahi nahi hai
		- shart-e-ijza  - agar theek ho jaye toh namaz firse padni hogi jo junoon ki halat me padhi thi
	- Ali RA se riwayat hai ke 3 tarah ke log mafoon qalam hai - sota hua aadmi jab tak wo bedaar na ho jaye, bachha jab tak baalig na ho jaye, aur pagal jab tak wo aqalmand na ho jaye
